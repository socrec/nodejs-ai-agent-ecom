# Frequently asked questions — Acme Snacks

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept checks or purchase orders.

## Discounts and promo codes
Promo codes are entered at checkout on the website. The chat assistant cannot apply or create discounts.

## Allergens
Every product page lists allergens. Our facility processes peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, milk and soy. Products are not certified allergen-free.

## Large orders
Orders over $500 are reviewed by our team before they are confirmed. Review usually takes less than one business day, and you will receive a confirmation email once approved.

## Changing or cancelling an order
Orders can be changed or cancelled until they ship, usually within 2 hours of placing them. Contact support as soon as possible.

## Contacting a human
Ask the assistant to connect you with support, or email help@acmesnacks.example. Support hours are 9 am to 6 pm Central, Monday through Friday.

## Subscriptions
We do not offer subscriptions yet.
