# Returns and refunds — Acme Snacks

## Return window
You may return unopened products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

## Opened products
Because snacks are perishable, opened bags cannot be returned. If a product is stale, defective or not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days and we will replace it or refund it without a return.

## How to start a return
Reply to your confirmation email or ask the assistant to connect you with support. We email a prepaid return label within 1 business day.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the return. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges
We do not process exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.
