# Acme Snacks: shipping policy

## Rates and free shipping
Standard shipping is a flat $5.99 per order. Orders with a subtotal of $50 or more ship free. We do not offer expedited shipping at this time.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm Central ship the same business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States. Alaska, Hawaii and PO boxes add 2 to 4 business days.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states and US territories. We do not ship internationally.

## Tracking
A tracking link is sent by email and SMS when your order leaves our warehouse. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we will open a carrier claim.

## Damaged or missing items
If your box arrives damaged or an item is missing, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will send a replacement at no cost.
