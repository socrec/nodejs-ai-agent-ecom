# Blue Harbor Beverages: frequently asked questions

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Apple Pay. Business customers can request net-30 invoicing by contacting support.

## Discounts and promo codes
Promo codes are entered at checkout on the website. The chat assistant cannot apply or create discounts.

## Caffeine and ingredients
Cold brew contains about 180 mg of caffeine per bottle. Green tea contains about 40 mg. Sparkling water, kombucha and sodas are caffeine-free. Kombucha contains trace alcohol (under 0.5%).

## Large orders
Orders over $500 are reviewed by our team before they are confirmed. Review usually takes less than one business day, and you will receive a confirmation email once approved.

## Changing or cancelling an order
Orders can be changed or cancelled until they ship, usually within 1 business day. Contact support as soon as possible.

## Contacting a human
Ask the assistant to connect you with support, or email help@blueharbor.example. Support hours are 8 am to 5 pm Pacific, Monday through Friday.

## Recycling
All cans and bottles are recyclable. Return 10 empty cases to our Seattle warehouse for a $5 store credit.
