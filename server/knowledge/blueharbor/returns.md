# Blue Harbor Beverages: returns and refunds

## Return window
Unopened cases may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method, less a $9.99 return shipping fee.

## Opened products
Opened cases and refrigerated items (kombucha, cold brew) cannot be returned. If a product is defective or arrived damaged, contact us within 14 days for a replacement.

## How to start a return
Ask the assistant to connect you with support or email help@blueharbor.example. We send a return label within 1 business day.

## Refund timing
Refunds are processed within 7 business days after the warehouse receives the return.

## Exchanges
We do not process exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.
