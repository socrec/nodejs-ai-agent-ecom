# Blue Harbor Beverages: shipping policy

## Rates and free shipping
Standard shipping is a flat $5.99 per order. Orders with a subtotal of $50 or more ship free. Expedited 2-day shipping is available for $14.99 on orders under 40 lb.

## Delivery times
Orders ship within 1 business day. Standard delivery takes 4 to 7 business days because beverages ship by ground freight. Kombucha ships in insulated packaging and only Monday through Wednesday.

## Where we ship
We ship to the 48 contiguous US states. We cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii, PO boxes or internationally because of the weight of liquids.

## Tracking
A tracking link is sent by email and SMS when your order leaves our warehouse.

## Damaged or leaking items
Glass bottles occasionally break in transit. Send a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the damaged units free of charge.
